Natural disasters

Ghi chú: warning, eruption, destruction, survivor là danh từ làm từ động từ: warn → warning, erupt → eruption, destroy → destruction, survive → survivor. The radio gave a warning — "một lời cảnh báo", chứ không phải "đang cảnh báo".

earthquake

/ˈɜːθkweɪk/1

nountrận động đất

nouncountable

a sudden violent shaking of the ground that can knock down buildings

trận động đất

damage

/ˈdæmɪdʒ/11

nounthiệt hại, hư hỏng

1

noununcountable

physical harm caused to something, which makes it less useful or valuable

thiệt hại, hư hỏng

  • The storm caused serious damage to the school roof.
  • There was little damage to the houses on the hill.
  • Engineers are checking the damage to the bridge.

cause damage · serious damage · damage to property

2

verbtransitive

to harm something so that it does not work properly or look as good as before

làm hư hại, gây thiệt hại

  • The flood damaged hundreds of motorbikes.
  • Strong winds damaged the roof but did not destroy the house.

badly damaged · damage crops

The noun is uncountable — say a lot of damage, never many damages. Compare with destroy: damage means to harm something partly, so it can be repaired; destroy means to ruin it completely.

destroy

/dɪˈstrɔɪ/12

verbphá huỷ, tiêu huỷ

verbtransitive

to damage something so badly that it no longer exists or can never be used again

phá huỷ, tiêu huỷ

  • The fire destroyed three houses in less than an hour.
  • The earthquake destroyed the old bridge completely.
  • Thousands of trees were destroyed by the landslide.

completely destroy · be destroyed by fire · destroy a building

Word family

  • destruction noun the act of destroying something, or the state of being destroyed sự phá huỷ, sự tàn pháThe typhoon left a trail of destruction along the coast.
  • destructive adjective causing a lot of damage có sức tàn pháIt was the most destructive storm in fifty years.

Watch out

There is no noun destroyment; the noun is destruction. Remember the difference: a damaged house can be repaired, a destroyed house cannot.

victim

/ˈvɪktɪm/16

nounnạn nhân

nouncountable

a person who has been hurt, killed or badly affected by an event or a crime

  • The victims of the earthquake need clean water and blankets.
  • Most of the flood victims are living in temporary shelters.
  • A fund was set up to help the victims of the landslide.

Watch out

A victim is someone harmed by the disaster — they may or may not have died. A survivor is someone who lived through it. Someone can be both: a victim who is now a survivor.
