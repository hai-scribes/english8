Project — A custom in your family

Record one custom properly, before the person who knows it best stops being able to explain it.

Step 1 — Choose and ask. Pick one custom your family keeps. Interview the oldest person you can reach about it. Ask at least these five questions:

  • What exactly do we do, and in what order?
  • Who taught you this?
  • Has anything about it changed since you were my age?
  • What must you never do?
  • What would be lost if we stopped?

Step 2 — Write it down. Produce a one-page record with three parts:

Part Content
What The custom, step by step, in order
Why The meaning, in your interviewee's own words
Warnings The taboos — what a guest must not do

Step 3 — Illustrate. Add one drawing or photograph, and label at least five objects in English (altar, incense, offering, tray, lantern …).

Step 4 — Present. Explain your custom aloud for one minute to someone at home, without reading word for word. Record it and listen back once, checking your articles.

Stretch: Ask a classmate from a different province or a different ethnic group about their version of the same kind of event. Write 60–80 words on one thing that is the same and one thing that is different.
