7.4

Error hunt

Find and correct six errors in this paragraph. Five are about articles; one is a subject–verb agreement slip.

Every year my family goes to my grandparents' house in a Ha Noi for Tet. We arrive on a last evening of the old year and wrap cakes until midnight. On the first morning my grandfather lights the first stick of incense, because he is oldest person in the family. Nobody sweep the floor that day — it is an taboo. We eat the breakfast together, and then the children receive lucky money.

  1. in a Ha Noi → in Ha Noi (no article with most cities)
  2. on a last evening → on the last evening (there is only one)
  3. he is oldest person → he is the oldest person (superlative)
  4. Nobody sweep → Nobody sweeps (subject–verb agreement)
  5. an taboo → a taboo (/t/ is a consonant sound)
  6. eat the breakfast → eat breakfast (meals take no article)

Corrected paragraph: Every year my family goes to my grandparents' house in Ha Noi for Tet. We arrive on the last evening of the old year and wrap cakes until midnight. On the first morning my grandfather lights the first stick of incense, because he is the oldest person in the family. Nobody sweeps the floor that day — it is a taboo. We eat breakfast together, and then the children receive lucky money.

Project — A custom in your family

Record one custom properly, before the person who knows it best stops being able to explain it.

Step 1 — Choose and ask. Pick one custom your family keeps. Interview the oldest person you can reach about it. Ask at least these five questions:

  • What exactly do we do, and in what order?
  • Who taught you this?
  • Has anything about it changed since you were my age?
  • What must you never do?
  • What would be lost if we stopped?

Step 2 — Write it down. Produce a one-page record with three parts:

Part Content
What The custom, step by step, in order
Why The meaning, in your interviewee's own words
Warnings The taboos — what a guest must not do

Step 3 — Illustrate. Add one drawing or photograph, and label at least five objects in English (altar, incense, offering, tray, lantern …).

Step 4 — Present. Explain your custom aloud for one minute to someone at home, without reading word for word. Record it and listen back once, checking your articles.

Stretch: Ask a classmate from a different province or a different ethnic group about their version of the same kind of event. Write 60–80 words on one thing that is the same and one thing that is different.
