Vocabulary — Science and technology

invention

/ɪnˈvenʃn/1
1

nouncountable

a machine, tool or idea that somebody has made for the first time

phát minh, sáng chế

  • The light bulb was one of the most useful inventions of the 19th century.
  • My little brother showed us his own invention: a robot made of paper cups.
  • Which invention has changed your daily life the most?

a great invention · a new invention · a useful invention

2

noununcountable

the act of making something that did not exist before

việc sáng chế, sự phát minh

  • The invention of the smartphone changed how we talk to each other.
  • Life became much faster after the invention of the aeroplane.

the invention of something · since the invention of

Word family

  • inventor noun a person who makes something new nhà phát minhThomas Edison was a famous American inventor.
  • inventive adjective good at thinking of new ideas sáng tạo, giàu óc sáng chếShe is very inventive and always finds a clever solution.

Watch out

Use the invention of + noun when you mean the moment something was first made: the invention of the printing press.

invent

/ɪnˈvent/2

verbtransitive

to make or design something completely new for the first time

phát minh, sáng chế

  • Who invented the telephone?
  • Students at our school invented a bag that charges a phone using sunlight.
  • Engineers are trying to invent a cheaper way to clean sea water.

invent a machine · invent a new method · be invented by

Word family

  • invention noun something new that has been made phát minhThe wheel is a very old invention.
  • inventor noun a person who invents things nhà phát minhThe inventor received a prize for her design.

Watch out

Invent = create something that never existed (Bell invented the telephone). Discover = find something that was already there (Scientists discovered a new planet). This is a very common learner mistake.

biometrics

/ˌbaɪəʊˈmetrɪks/3

noununcountable

the use of body features such as fingerprints, faces or eyes to check who a person is

sinh trắc học

  • Biometrics is now used at most international airports.
  • Our new phone uses biometrics instead of a password.
  • Biometrics makes it harder for someone to steal your identity.

use biometrics · biometrics technology

Word family

  • biometric adjective connected with measuring features of the human body thuộc sinh trắc họcThe school gate has a biometric scanner.

Watch out

Biometrics ends in -s but takes a singular verb: Biometrics is used in banks. (like physics, economics). Before a noun, use the adjective biometric: a biometric passport.

fingerprint

/ˈfɪŋɡəprɪnt/4

nouncountable

the mark made by the lines on the end of your finger, which is different for every person

dấu vân tay

  • I unlock my laptop with my fingerprint.
  • The police found a fingerprint on the glass.
  • No two people in the world have the same fingerprints.

take someone's fingerprints · a fingerprint scanner · leave a fingerprint

Watch out

Written as one word. The machine that reads it is a fingerprint scanner or fingerprint reader.

iris

/ˈaɪərɪs/5
1

nouncountable

the coloured ring around the black centre of your eye

mống mắt

  • The camera scans your iris before the door opens.
  • Her irises are a very dark brown.
  • Iris recognition is faster than typing a password.

iris scanner · iris recognition

2

nouncountable

a tall garden flower with long thin leaves, usually purple, blue or yellow

hoa diên vĩ (hoa iris)

  • My grandmother grows purple irises beside the pond.
  • There were yellow irises all along the river.

grow irises · a bunch of irises

Watch out

Plural: irises. In technology texts you will almost always meet the eye meaning, as in iris scanning.

recognition

/ˌrekəɡˈnɪʃn/6
1

noununcountable

the act of knowing who or what somebody or something is, especially by a machine

sự nhận dạng, sự nhận diện

  • Face recognition lets you pay without a card.
  • Voice recognition software can type what you say.
  • Recognition of your fingerprint takes less than a second.

facial recognition · voice recognition · speech recognition

2

noununcountable

public praise for what somebody has done well

sự công nhận, sự ghi nhận

  • The young scientist finally got the recognition she deserved.
  • He received a medal in recognition of his work on clean energy.

gain recognition · in recognition of

Word family

  • recognise verb to know somebody or something because you have seen it before nhận ra, nhận diệnThe app recognises my face even in the dark.

Watch out

British spelling recognise; American spelling recognize. Both are pronounced the same.

device

/dɪˈvaɪs/7

nouncountable

a machine or piece of equipment made to do one particular job

thiết bị

  • This small device records your voice and turns it into text.
  • Please switch off all electronic devices during the test.
  • In 2050, we may wear a device that translates every language.

an electronic device · a mobile device · use a device

Watch out

Device is the noun; devise (with an s) is the verb meaning to invent. A device is any tool or machine, often serious or technical; a gadget is usually small, clever and a bit fun — a phone is a device, a tiny camera pen is a gadget.

artificial intelligence

/ˌɑːtɪfɪʃl ɪnˈtelɪdʒəns/8

noununcountable

the ability of computers to do things that normally need human thinking, such as learning and making decisions

trí tuệ nhân tạo

  • Artificial intelligence can translate a whole book in seconds.
  • Doctors use artificial intelligence to read X-ray pictures.
  • Our teacher explained how artificial intelligence learns from data.

use artificial intelligence · artificial intelligence technology

Watch out

Uncountable — no a/an and no plural. It is usually shortened to AI: an AI tool, AI is changing schools. Say the two letters: /ˌeɪ ˈaɪ/.

automate

/ˈɔːtəmeɪt/9

verbtransitive

to make a machine or computer do a job that people used to do

tự động hoá

  • The factory has automated most of its packing work.
  • We can automate the watering of the school garden.
  • Many boring office tasks are now automated.

automate a process · fully automated · automate a task

Word family

  • automation noun the use of machines to do work instead of people sự tự động hoáAutomation has changed the way cars are built.
  • automatic adjective working by itself without a person controlling it tự độngThe lights in the corridor are automatic.

Watch out

Automatic describes a thing; automatically describes how something happens: The door opens automatically.

nanolearning

/ˈnænəʊlɜːnɪŋ/10

noununcountable

a way of studying in which you learn a very small amount at a time, usually in lessons of a few minutes

học siêu ngắn (học theo từng đoạn rất ngắn)

  • Nanolearning is popular with students who are always busy.
  • With nanolearning, five minutes on the bus is enough for one lesson.
  • Our app uses nanolearning to teach ten new words a week.

a nanolearning app · use nanolearning

Watch out

A very new word, so many dictionaries do not have it yet. Nano- means extremely small, so nanolearning means learning in very short bursts — often two to five minutes on a phone. Compare microlearning, which means almost the same thing.

efficient

/ɪˈfɪʃnt/11

adjective

doing a job well without wasting time, money or energy

hiệu quả, có năng suất

  • Electric trains are more efficient than old diesel ones.
  • This robot is efficient: it does in one hour what took us a day.
  • We need a more efficient way to sort the rubbish.

energy efficient · highly efficient · an efficient method

Word family

  • efficiency noun the quality of working well without waste hiệu suất, sự hiệu quảThe new engine improved the car's efficiency.
  • efficiently adverb in a way that does not waste time or energy một cách hiệu quảThe team worked quickly and efficiently.

Watch out

Efficient = no waste of time or energy. Effective = it gives the result you want. A method can be effective but not efficient if it takes too long.

innovative

/ˈɪnəvətɪv/12

adjective

using new ideas or new ways of doing things

đổi mới, sáng tạo

  • The team won the prize for their innovative water filter.
  • Our school has a very innovative way of teaching science.
  • Viet Nam has many innovative young start-ups.

an innovative idea · an innovative design · highly innovative

Word family

  • innovation noun a new idea or method, or the making of new ideas sự đổi mới, cải tiếnThis app is the biggest innovation in online learning this year.
  • innovate verb to introduce new ideas or methods đổi mới, cải tiếnCompanies must innovate or they will lose customers.

breakthrough

/ˈbreɪkθruː/13

nouncountable

an important discovery or change that helps you make progress after a long time

bước đột phá

  • Scientists have made a breakthrough in the treatment of malaria.
  • The new battery is a real breakthrough for electric cars.
  • After months of failure, they finally had a breakthrough.

make a breakthrough · a major breakthrough · a scientific breakthrough

Watch out

The noun is one word, breakthrough; the verb phrase is two words: break through. We usually say make a breakthrough, not ~~do~~ a breakthrough.

laboratory

/ləˈbɒrətri/14

nouncountable

a room or building where scientists do tests and research

phòng thí nghiệm

  • The samples were sent to a laboratory for testing.
  • You must wear glasses and gloves in the laboratory.
  • Our school built a new computer laboratory last year.

a science laboratory · work in a laboratory · a laboratory test

Watch out

The short, everyday form is lab: the school lab, lab coat. Stress differs: British la-BOR-a-try, American LAB-ra-tory.

experiment

/ɪkˈsperɪmənt/15
1

nouncountable

a scientific test done to find out what happens or to prove an idea

cuộc thí nghiệm, thực nghiệm

  • We did an experiment with salt and ice in class today.
  • The experiment showed that the plants grew faster in blue light.
  • Her first experiment failed, so she tried again.

do an experiment · carry out an experiment · a simple experiment

2

verb+ with / on

to try new things or do scientific tests to see what happens

thí nghiệm, thử nghiệm

  • The class experimented with different kinds of soil.
  • Scientists are experimenting with a new kind of plastic.
  • I like to experiment with recipes at the weekend.

experiment with something · experiment on animals

Word family

  • experimental adjective connected with tests, or still being tested mang tính thử nghiệmThe hospital is using an experimental drug.

Watch out

The noun and the verb are spelt the same but stressed differently in careful speech, and we say do or carry out an experiment — not ~~make an experiment~~.

1

to take the place of somebody or something else

thay thế cho

  • Will robots replace teachers in the future?

2

verbtransitive

benefit

/ˈbenɪfɪt/17
1

nouncountable

2

hưởng lợi; mang lại lợi ích cho

drawback

/ˈdrɔːbæk/18

nouncountable

a bad point or problem that makes something less good

nhược điểm, mặt hạn chế

  • The main drawback of the robot is its high price.
  • Studying online has one big drawback: you miss your friends.

Watch out

Drawback is the opposite of benefit / advantage. It is countable, and it is often used in the pattern The (main) drawback of X is that…

Pronunciation — Stress in statements and questions

English does not give every word the same weight. Some words are said louder, longer and higher; the rest are squeezed. This is sentence stress.

Content words — STRESSED Function words — weak
Which words nouns, main verbs, adjectives, adverbs, question words, not articles, prepositions, auxiliary verbs, pronouns, conjunctions
Examples robot, invent, efficient, quickly, what, why, not a, an, the, in, of, to, is, do, can, I, he, it, and, but
Why they carry the meaning you can guess them from grammar

Say this sentence. Only the CAPITAL words get a beat:

The SCIENTISTS are FINISHING their EXPERIMENT in the LABORATORY.

Now the contrast the unit is about — statements vs questions.

Type Example (stress in CAPITALS) Voice at the end
Statement The ROBOT can READ my FINGERPRINT. falls ↘
Wh- question WHAT did the TEACHER SAY? falls ↘
Yes/No question Can a MACHINE REPLACE a TEACHER? rises ↗
Short answer Yes, it CAN. / No, it CAN'T. falls ↘

Three things to notice:

  1. The Wh- word is stressed — WHAT, WHY, WHERE, HOW — and the voice still falls at the end, exactly like a statement.
  2. A Yes/No question starts with a weak auxiliary (Can → /kən/, Do → /də/) and rises at the end.
  3. That same auxiliary becomes strong and stressed in a short answer: Yes, it CAN. Never weaken the last word of a short answer.

Mẹo: Đừng đọc đều đều từng từ. Hãy gõ nhịp bằng tay: mỗi từ nội dung (danh từ, động từ chính, tính từ, trạng từ, từ để hỏi) là một nhịp; các từ chức năng đọc lướt qua. Câu trần thuật và câu hỏi Wh- hạ giọng ở cuối; câu hỏi Yes/No lên giọng.

One new-information word per sentence — and unstress the rest

Go back through exercise 2.4. In each sentence, circle exactly one word: the one carrying information the listener does not already have. Say the sentence with that word prominent and everything else flattened. Record it on your phone and listen for whether the rest really did get out of the way.

Across 280 benchmark speaking performances, the proportion of words a speaker makes prominent correlates −0.42 with rated level: lower-rated speakers made roughly 39% of their words prominent, the highest-rated about 31%. Stressing more words does not sound more emphatic; it sounds flatter, because nothing stands out when everything does.

Two warnings that travel with this. The strongest correlate in that study was speech rate (0.67) — and the researchers call it "the worst teaching target", because speeding up to imitate a score is exactly the wrong response. And this is Tier-2 research on how candidates behave, never a rule of the test. No pronunciation score exists here and none will: examiner agreement on pronunciation is the weakest of the four criteria, and nothing in this course will ever put a number on yours.

Tiếng Việt: Mỗi câu chỉ nhấn một từ — từ mang thông tin mới. Nhấn càng nhiều từ, câu càng phẳng. Khoá học này không chấm điểm phát âm.
