Grammar check

MarkedShort answer

Rewrite each one in reported speech. Write the whole sentence, beginning with the reporting verb in brackets, and change everything else that has to change — the verb, the pronouns, and the time and place words. that is optional.

  • Spelling counts.
  • British and American spellings are both accepted.
  1. Tí said (that) he was reading about biometrics./Ti said (that) he was reading about biometrics.
  2. The club told us (that) they had built a robot.
  3. Khoa told me (that) he would send me the file the next day./Khoa told me (that) he would send me the file the following day. (you is the person reporting, so it becomes me)
  4. My teacher told me (that) I had to finish it that day./My teacher told me (that) I had to finish that that day. (must becomes had to)
  5. Đạt said (that) he couldn't come to the laboratory./Đạt said (that) he could not come to the laboratory./Dat said (that) he couldn't come to the laboratory./Dat said (that) he could not come to the laboratory.
  6. Cô Yến said (that) the Earth goes round the Sun./Co Yen said (that) the Earth goes round the Sun./Cô Yến said (that) the Earth went round the Sun./Co Yen said (that) the Earth went round the Sun. (a general truth needs no backshift, though the past is accepted too)

Thảo said (that) she had seen that device there the day before./Thao said (that) she had seen that device there the day before./Thảo said (that) she had seen that device there the previous day./Thao said (that) she had seen that device there the previous day. (this becomes that and here becomes there)

7.4

Error hunt

Find and correct six errors in this paragraph. Four are about reported speech, one is a pronoun that was never shifted, and one is a subject–verb agreement slip.

One mark is left on the wall. Bống told that the keeper had underlined the rule. He said us that we should shut the notebook and go home. Thảo said that I wanted to call the keeper back, which was true of all three of us. Khoa told me that he will read the last page again tomorrow. His brother say that nobody upriver remembers the mill.

MarkedShort answer

Read the paragraph again and find the errors yourself — their places are not given. Write the correction only: the word or words that should replace the wrong ones, in the order the errors appear. Spelling counts.

  • Spelling counts.
  • British and American spellings are both accepted.
  1. told us that/told us (tell needs a person after it)
  2. he told us (said us → told us)
  3. she wanted (the pronoun shifts — she is reporting what she said about herself)
  4. he would read (will backshifts to would)
  5. the next day/the following day (tomorrow shifts to the next day)
  6. his brother said (say → said — and it is a report)

Project — An invention file

Build a one-page file on a piece of technology you actually use.

Step 1 — Choose. Pick one device, app or invention you use at least three times a week. Do not pick something you have only read about.

Step 2 — Research and record. Fill in the table. Leave a box empty rather than guessing — an empty box is honest, a made-up fact is not.

Question Your notes
What is it, and what does it do?
What did people do before it existed?
Three benefits
Two drawbacks
Who in your family uses it most?

Step 3 — Interview. Ask three people this question: "Could you live without it? Why?" Write down their exact words, then report each answer in reported speech:

Person Their exact words Reported
1 " " said / told me that…
2 " "
3 " "

Step 4 — Write it up. Write a report of 120–150 words: what the technology is, its benefits, its drawbacks, and what your three people said. Use at least five reported statements.

Step 5 — Present it. Present your file aloud for ninety seconds without reading word for word. Record it, then listen back once and check your sentence stress: are the content words louder than the function words?

Stretch: Now argue the other side. Write 60 words explaining why someone might refuse to use your chosen technology at all. A good opinion paragraph builds the opposite case before knocking it down.
