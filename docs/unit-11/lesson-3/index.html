A Closer Look 2

Grammar — Reported speech: statements

When you tell someone what another person said, you do not repeat their exact words. You report them.

Direct: Thảo said, "I am tired."
Reported: Thảo said that she was tired.

Four things can change. Learn them in this order.

(a) The reporting verb — say or tell

This is the first decision, and the one most learners get wrong.

Verb Pattern Example
say say (that) + clause — no person He said that the test was easy.
tell tell + person + (that) + clause — person required He told me that the test was easy.

✅ She said that the robot was expensive. ✅ She told us that the robot was expensive. ❌ She said us that the robot was expensive. ❌ She told that the robot was expensive.

that itself is optional in both: He said (that) he was busy.

(b) Backshift — the verb moves one tense back

Direct speech → Reported speech
present simple — works → past simple — worked
present continuous — is working → past continuous — was working
present perfect — has worked → past perfect — had worked
past simple — worked → past perfect — had worked
will → would
can → could
may → might
must → had to

"I write code." → He said he wrote code. "I am testing it." → He said he was testing it. "I have finished." → He said he had finished. "I finished it." → He said he had finished it. "I will call." → He said he would call. "I can help." → He said he could help. "It may rain." → He said it might rain. "You must wait." → He told us we had to wait.

Note that had worked covers two direct tenses — present perfect and past simple both land there. Also note that would, could, might and should do not change: they are already past forms.

(c) Pronouns shift

The speaker changes, so the pronouns must change with them.

Direct → Reported
I → he / she
we → they
my → his / her
our → their
you (= the listener) → me / us

Thảo said, "I have lost my notes." → Thảo said that she had lost her notes. The teacher said, "We will help you." → The teacher told us that they would help us.

(d) Time and place words shift

You are reporting later, and somewhere else.

Direct → Reported
now → then
today → that day
tonight → that night
tomorrow → the next day / the following day
yesterday → the day before / the previous day
next week → the following week
last week → the week before
here → there
this → that
these → those

Tí said, "I bought this device yesterday." → Tí said that he had bought that device the day before.

(e) When NOT to backshift

If the statement is a general truth that is still true now, you may keep the present tense. Vietnamese textbooks accept both, but the present sounds more natural.

Cô Yến said that water boils at 100 degrees Celsius. ✅ She told us that no two fingerprints are the same. ✅

The same applies when the situation has not changed yet: He said he lives in Hue — and he still does.

⚠️ Bẫy thường gặp: Hai lỗi này chiếm phần lớn bài sai của học sinh lớp 8.

1. Nhầm say và tell. tell bắt buộc có tân ngữ chỉ người ngay sau nó; say thì không có. ❌ He told that he was busy. → ✅ He said that he was busy. ❌ He said me that he was busy. → ✅ He told me that he was busy.

2. Quên đổi đại từ. Lùi thì xong rồi vẫn để nguyên I, my, we. ❌ Thảo said that I was doing my homework. (nghĩa đã sai hoàn toàn!) → ✅ Thảo said that she was doing her homework.

MarkedCorrect the mistake

Each sentence has exactly one mistake. Write only the words that should replace the wrong ones — not the whole sentence. Four of these are about say and tell; the other four are about backshift.

  • Spelling counts.
  • British and American spellings are both accepted.
  1. said/told me/told us (tell needs a person after it, and there is none here)
  2. told me/said (say never takes a person)
  3. she (the pronoun shifts out of the first person)
  4. told us (a person after the verb means it must be tell)
  5. would (will backshifts to would)
  6. had (the present perfect backshifts to the past perfect)
  7. had bought (the past simple backshifts to the past perfect)
  8. could (can backshifts to could)

About you

Write true reported sentences. Use a different reporting verb pattern each time, and remember the pronoun shift.

  1. Something a teacher said to your class this month. → My teacher told us that
  2. Something a family member said to you yesterday. →
  3. Something you said about your own plans for next week. → I said that
  4. A general truth a science teacher has told you (do not backshift). →
  5. Something a friend said about a device or app they like. →

Answers will vary. Check: (1) told us that — a person after told; (2) a person after told / no person after said; (3) I said that with the pronoun and time word shifted; (4) present tense kept, because it is a general truth; (5) the pronoun changed from I/my to he/she/his/her.
