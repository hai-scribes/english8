Part A — Prepositions of place

These little words tell us where something is.

Preposition Meaning (Nghĩa) Example
in inside something The ticket is in the envelope.
on touching a surface My phone is on the table.
at at a point / a place you use We met at the gate.
under lower than, often covered The charger is under the sofa.
above higher than, not touching There is a clock above the door.
between in the middle of two things The café is between a bank and a shop.
next to immediately beside Sit next to me.
in front of before something, facing it She stood in front of the screen.
behind at the back of The bin is behind the door.
opposite facing, across from The museum is opposite the post office.

✅ The museum is opposite the post office. (across the road) ✅ The café is next to the bookshop. (side by side) ❌ The museum is in opposite the post office. ❌ The café is next the bookshop. — you must keep to.

Part A — Prepositions of time

Use With Examples
in months, years, seasons, parts of the day in May · in 2025 · in winter · in the morning / the afternoon / the evening
on days of the week, dates, a named day + part of day on Monday · on 3 September · on Friday evening
at clock times, at night, festivals, at the weekend at 9.15 · at half past four · at night · at Tet · at the weekend

✅ We have English on Friday. ✅ The talk starts at nine o'clock. ✅ She was born in 2011. ✅ I read in the evening but never at night.

⚠️ Bẫy thường gặp: Đây là bốn lỗi mà học sinh Việt Nam mắc nhiều nhất.

  1. ❌ in Monday → ✅ on Monday. Thứ và ngày tháng luôn đi với on.
  2. ❌ on 2025 → ✅ in 2025. Năm, tháng, mùa luôn đi với in.
  3. ❌ in night → ✅ at night. Nhớ cặp đối lập: in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening NHƯNG at night. Đây là ngoại lệ duy nhất, phải học thuộc.
  4. ❌ in nine o'clock → ✅ at nine o'clock. Giờ đồng hồ luôn đi với at.

Mẹo nhớ: in = khoảng dài (năm, tháng, buổi) → on = một ngày cụ thể → at = một điểm thời gian. Càng ngắn thì càng dùng at.

Part B — Possessive pronouns

A possessive adjective comes before a noun. A possessive pronoun replaces the noun completely.

Subject Possessive adjective (+ noun) Possessive pronoun (no noun)
I my phone mine
you your phone yours
he his phone his
she her phone hers
we our phone ours
they their phone theirs

✅ This is my ticket. = This ticket is mine. ✅ Her network is faster than ours. ✅ Whose phone is this? — It's theirs.

⚠️ Bẫy thường gặp: Hai lỗi phải tránh bằng mọi giá.

Lỗi 1 — đại từ sở hữu KHÔNG bao giờ có danh từ đứng sau. ❌ This is mine book. → ✅ This is my book. / ✅ This book is mine. ❌ That is hers camera. → ✅ That is her camera. / ✅ That camera is hers.

Lỗi 2 — KHÔNG có dấu nháy đơn (') trong đại từ sở hữu. ❌ your's, her's, their's, our's → ✅ yours, hers, theirs, ours Phân biệt cho kỹ: - its = của nó (sở hữu) · it's = it is / it has ✅ The phone lost its signal. ✅ It's a very old phone. - theirs = của họ · there's = there is ✅ The red bag is theirs. ✅ There's a bag on the chair.

Correct the mistake

MarkedCorrect the mistake

Each sentence has exactly one mistake. Tap the wrong word, then choose what should replace it.

  • One mark each. Nothing is part-marked.
  1. in → on (a date takes *on*)
  2. in → at (a clock time takes *at*)
  3. on → in (a part of the day takes *in*)
  4. in → at (*night* is the exception: *at night*)
  5. mine → my (a possessive **pronoun** stands alone; before a noun you need the adjective)
  6. Their's → Theirs (possessive pronouns never take an apostrophe)
  7. your's → yours (possessive pronouns never take an apostrophe)
  8. Its → It's (*it's* = *it is*; *its* is the possessive)
