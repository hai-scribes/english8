Unit 12 · Life on Other Planets · Lesson 3 of 7

A Closer Look 2

Grammar — Reported speech: questions

In Unit 11 you reported statements:

"I live near the observatory." → She said (that) she lived near the observatory.

This unit does the same job with questions. The backshift you learned in Unit 11 is unchanged. What is new is the word order.

The one rule that carries this whole lesson

A reported question is not a question any more. It is a statement about a question. So it loses all three things that make an English question look like a question:

A real question has… A reported question has…
the auxiliary do / does / did no do / does / did
inversion (are you, did he) normal statement order (you are, he did)
a question mark ? a full stop .

❌ He asked me where did I live. ✅ He asked me where I lived.

❌ She asked what does a planet need? ✅ She asked what a planet needed.

(a) Yes/No questions → asked (me) if / whether

There is no question word, so you supply one: if or whether. They mean the same thing here; whether is slightly more formal.

Direct Reported
"Do you believe in aliens?" She asked me if I believed in aliens.
"Is Mars habitable?" He asked whether Mars was habitable.
"Did they launch the spacecraft?" I asked if they had launched the spacecraft.
"Can you see Saturn tonight?" She asked whether I could see Saturn that night.

Notice Do you believe → I believed: the do disappears completely and its tense moves onto the main verb.

(b) Wh- questions → asked (me) + question word + statement order

Keep the question word. Everything after it becomes a plain statement.

Direct Reported
"What does a planet need?" She asked what a planet needed.
"Where do you live?" He asked me where I lived.
"When will humans land on Mars?" They asked when humans would land on Mars.
"Why are you laughing?" She asked me why I was laughing.
"How did the crew survive?" He asked how the crew had survived.

Ghi chú: Nếu điều được nói vẫn luôn đúng (một sự thật khoa học), bạn có thể giữ nguyên thì: She asked why Mars is red. Ở lớp 8, cả hai cách đều được chấp nhận, nhưng hãy lùi thì cho chắc.

⚠️ Bẫy thường gặp: Lỗi phổ biến nhất của học sinh Việt Nam là giữ lại hình thức câu hỏi trong câu tường thuật: ❌ He asked me where did I live? ✅ He asked me where I lived. Hãy kiểm tra ba thứ mỗi lần viết: (1) không có do/does/did, (2) chủ ngữ đứng trước động từ, (3) kết thúc bằng dấu chấm, không phải dấu chấm hỏi. Ngoài ra, đừng dùng if khi câu hỏi đã có từ để hỏi: ❌ She asked if what I wanted. ✅ She asked what I wanted.

Report these Yes/No questions

MarkedShort answer

Write the whole reported sentence, beginning with the words in brackets. It ends in a full stop, not a question mark. Either if or whether is accepted.

  • Spelling counts.
  • British and American spellings are both accepted.
  1. She asked me if I liked science fiction./She asked me whether I liked science fiction.
  2. He asked if there was water on Mars./He asked whether there was water on Mars.

  5. Khoa asked me if I was coming to the club the next day./Khoa asked me whether I was coming to the club the next day./Khoa asked me if I was coming to the club the following day./Khoa asked me whether I was coming to the club the following day. (tomorrow shifts as well as the verb)
  6. My teacher asked if I had ever used a telescope./My teacher asked whether I had ever used a telescope.
3.4

Report these Wh- questions

MarkedShort answer

Write the whole reported sentence, beginning with the words in brackets. The question word stays; the do/does/did goes, and the subject comes back in front of the verb.

  • Spelling counts.
  • British and American spellings are both accepted.
  1. He asked me where I lived.
  2. She asked us what a planet needed to support life./She asked us what a planet needs to support life. (this one is a general truth, so the present tense is accepted too)
  3. They asked when humans would land on Mars.
  4. My aunt asked me why I was studying astronomy.
  5. The reporter asked how the creatures had survived the winter.
  6. Hùng asked who had launched the first spacecraft./Hung asked who had launched the first spacecraft.
  7. I asked the shop assistant how much a telescope cost.
3.5

Correct the mistake

MarkedCorrect the mistake

Each sentence has exactly one mistake. Write only the words that should replace the wrong ones — not the whole sentence. Every mistake here is in the reported question itself — the word order, the tense, or the word that joins the two halves.

  • Spelling counts.
  • British and American spellings are both accepted.
  1. I had put/I put (a reported question has no did, and the verb backshifts)
  2. what (a question word does the joining on its own; if has no business there)
  3. I wanted (no do, statement order, and the tense steps back)
  4. the club would meet (subject before verb, and will backshifts)
  5. I was (statement order, so the subject comes first)
  6. if/whether (a Yes/No question is joined by if or whether, never that)
  7. we knew (no do, and the present steps back to the past)
  8. was (the present steps back to the past)
