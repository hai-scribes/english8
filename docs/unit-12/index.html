How a lesson works
  1. Read the teaching part at the top: the example, the table, the rule.
  2. Do the exercises. Choose, tap or build your answer, then press Check answers. You see what was right and why.
  3. Press “Finish lesson” at the bottom. That records it and takes you back to Today.

Practice this unit's words

All 25 words from Lesson 2, asked in five ways: from the meaning, from the word, from what you hear, and — most often — inside a phrase the word really goes in. Anything you get wrong comes straight back, then again in a week.

Unit test

Every word once, with no feedback until the end. Before each answer is checked you say how sure you were, and the result shows whether feeling sure actually meant being right.
