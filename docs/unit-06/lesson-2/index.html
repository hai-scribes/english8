Vocabulary — Lifestyles

lifestyle

/ˈlaɪfstaɪl/1

nouncountable

the way in which a person or group lives, including their habits, work and interests

lối sống, phong cách sống

  • City life and country life are two very different lifestyles.
  • He changed his lifestyle and started cycling to work.
  • A healthy lifestyle includes sleep as well as exercise.

a healthy lifestyle · an active lifestyle · change your lifestyle

Watch out

Written as one word. Careful: lifestyle is how you live; life is the fact of living.

traditional

/trəˈdɪʃənl/2

adjective

following ideas, customs or methods that have existed for a long time

truyền thống, cổ truyền

  • They wore traditional costumes for the festival.
  • Weaving is a traditional craft in this village.
  • Traditional music is played on the gong.

traditional dress · a traditional festival · traditional music

Word family

  • tradition noun a belief or custom passed from one generation to the next truyền thốngIt is a tradition to visit relatives at Tet.
  • traditionally adverb in the way that has been usual for a long time theo truyền thốngTraditionally, the house faces south.

modern

/ˈmɒdn/3

adjective

of the present time, or using the newest ideas and technology

hiện đại

  • Modern life moves much faster.
  • The village now has a modern school.
  • He prefers modern furniture to antiques.

modern technology · modern life · the modern world

Word family

  • modernise verb to make something more modern hiện đại hoáThe government plans to modernise the railway.

Watch out

Opposite: traditional or old-fashioned.

sedentary

/ˈsedntri/4

adjective

involving a lot of sitting and little exercise

ít vận động, ngồi nhiều

  • A sedentary lifestyle is bad for your health.
  • Office work is often sedentary.
  • Too much screen time makes children sedentary.

a sedentary lifestyle · sedentary work

Watch out

Opposite: active. Stress the first syllable: SED-en-try.

active

/ˈæktɪv/5

adjective

doing a lot of physical exercise or many activities

năng động, tích cực vận động

  • My grandfather is still very active at eighty.
  • Try to stay active during the holidays.
  • She leads an active life.

stay active · an active lifestyle · physically active

Word family

  • activity noun a thing that you do, often for enjoyment hoạt độngSwimming is my favourite activity.

Watch out

Opposite: sedentary or inactive.

rural

/ˈrʊərəl/6

adjective

connected with the countryside rather than the town

thuộc về nông thôn

  • Rural life is quieter but there are fewer jobs.
  • Many families have moved away from rural areas.
  • The school serves five rural villages.

a rural area · the rural population · rural life

Watch out

Opposite: urban. Pronounced /ˈrʊərəl/: ROOR-uhl.

urban

/ˈɜːbən/7

adjective

connected with a town or city

thuộc về thành thị, đô thị

  • Urban areas are growing quickly.
  • Urban life offers more choice but less space.
  • Air quality is worse in urban districts.

an urban area · urban life · urban population

Watch out

Opposite: rural. Do not confuse with urgent.

adjectivebefore a noun

community

/kəˈmjuːnəti/9

/əˈdæpt/11

verbintransitive, + to

adapt to a new situation · adapt quickly

2

verbtransitive

to change something so that it is suitable for a new use

sửa đổi cho phù hợp, chuyển thể

/ˌwel ˈbiːɪŋ/18

Pronunciation — /br/ and /pr/

Two consonant clusters that are easy to swap. The two sounds are made in exactly the same place — both lips — so only one thing separates them: the voice. That is where the difficulty actually sits, and the bridge at the end of this block gives the measurement behind that claim.

/br/ — as in bright /pr/ — as in price
Voice voiced — your throat buzzes on the /b/ voiceless — no buzz on the /p/
Air no puff of air a small puff of air on the /p/
Examples bright, brother, bring, break, brave, brush, umbrella, celebrate price, practice, protect, prefer, print, produce, surprise, improve

Minimal pairs — the same word except for /b/ or /p/. Say each pair slowly.

/br/ Nghĩa /pr/ Nghĩa
bride /braɪd/ cô dâu pride /praɪd/ niềm tự hào
bray /breɪ/ tiếng lừa kêu pray /preɪ/ cầu nguyện
brawn /brɔːn/ sức cơ bắp prawn /prɔːn/ con tôm
breach /briːtʃ/ chỗ vỡ, sự vi phạm preach /priːtʃ/ thuyết giảng
brim /brɪm/ vành (mũ, cốc) prim /prɪm/ kiểu cách, cứng nhắc

Mẹo: Đặt tay lên cổ họng. Với /br/ (bright) bạn sẽ thấy rung ngay từ đầu; với /pr/ (price) thì không rung, và có một luồng hơi nhỏ bật ra. Thử đặt tờ giấy trước miệng: price làm giấy động, bright thì không.

Go further

This is a voicing drill, not a cluster drill

Work the voice and the puff of air, not the consonants. Put a hand on your throat: /b/ buzzes, /p/ does not. Hold a strip of paper in front of your mouth: /p/ moves it, /b/ does not.

Here is why that is the drill. When these clusters go wrong, the usual slip is not a missing consonant — it is /p/ coming out as /b/, the voicing changing while both consonants stay put. Fixing the voicing fixes the cluster; drilling the cluster does not fix the voicing.

Tiếng Việt: Đây là bài luyện thanh (voicing) và hơi bật (aspiration), không phải bài luyện cụm phụ âm. Lỗi phổ biến nhất là đổi /p/ thành /b/, chứ không phải lược bỏ phụ âm.

Answers will vary (spoken). Check: your throat buzzes on the br of brother, bright, bring, brush, celebrate, umbrella — at the very start in the first four, and in the middle of celebrate and umbrella — and does not buzz on the pr of practise, improve, price, print.
