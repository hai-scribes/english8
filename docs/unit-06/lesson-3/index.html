A Closer Look 2

Grammar — The future simple: will and won't

Form: subject + will + bare infinitive (no to, no -s, no -ing).

Statement
+ I / you / he / she / it / we / they will go. ('ll go)
− I / you / he / she / it / we / they will not go. (won't go)
? Will you go?

Short answers: Yes, I will. / No, I won't. — never Yes, I will go. in a short answer, and never No, I willn't.

✅ She will move to the city next year. ❌ She will moves… · She will to move… · She won't goes.

We use will for three things at this level:

Use Example Nghĩa
Prediction — what we think, guess or expect I think it will rain this afternoon. dự đoán
Spontaneous decision — decided at the moment of speaking That bag looks heavy. I'll carry it for you. quyết định tức thì
Promise / refusal I won't tell anyone, I promise. lời hứa / lời từ chối

Useful signposts: tomorrow, next week/year, in 2035, soon, later, one day, I think…, I'm sure…, probably, perhaps.

Ghi chú: probably đứng sau will nhưng trước won't: She will probably come. / She probably won't come.

Grammar — The first conditional

We use it for a real, possible future situation and its result.

If + present simple, will + bare infinitive

If-clause (condition) Main clause (result)
If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
If you eat more vegetables, you will feel better.
If I don't hurry, I will miss the bus.

Clause order and the comma. Both orders mean exactly the same thing; the comma depends on which clause comes first:

✅ If you study hard, you will pass. — if-clause first → use a comma ✅ You will pass if you study hard. — main clause first → no comma

unless = if … not. After unless, the verb is positive.

✅ Unless you hurry, you will miss the bus. = If you don't hurry, you will miss the bus. ❌ Unless you don't hurry, you will miss the bus. — double negative.

when vs if in a future clause. Both take the present simple, but they mean different things:

Meaning Example
when it is certain to happen When I am eighteen, I will vote.
if it is only possible If I pass, I will celebrate.

❌ When I will be eighteen… — never will after when in a future clause.

⚠️ Bẫy thường gặp: Tiếng Việt nói "Nếu ngày mai trời sẽ mưa…", nên học sinh Việt Nam hay viết If it will rain tomorrow… — SAI. Trong tiếng Anh, mệnh đề if không bao giờ có will, dù nói về tương lai: ✅ If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home. ❌ If it will rain tomorrow, we will stay at home. Quy tắc ngắn gọn: chỉ một chữ will, và nó nằm ở mệnh đề kết quả. Điều này áp dụng cho cả unless và when: ❌ Unless it will stop…, ❌ When she will arrive…

First conditional

Put the verbs into the correct form.

  1. If it (rain) tomorrow, we (not go) to the beach.
  2. If you (eat) more vegetables, you (feel) better.
  3. We (miss) the bus if we (not leave) now.
  4. Unless she (practise) every day, she (not improve).
  5. If the internet (be) slow, I (not join) the online lesson.
  6. When I (be) eighteen, I (move) to the city.

  2. practises / won't improve 5. is / won't join 6. am / will move

3.5

About you

Write true sentences about yourself. Use the structure given each time.

  1. (a promise, with won't)
  2. (If + present simple, will…)
  3. (Unless…)
  4. (When I am older, I will…)

Answers will vary. Check: no will anywhere in an if- or when-clause; unless followed by a positive verb; a bare infinitive after will/won't; a comma only when the if-clause comes first.
