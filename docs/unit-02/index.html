Practice this unit's words

All 36 words from Lesson 2, asked in five ways: from the meaning, from the word, from what you hear, and — most often — inside a phrase the word really goes in. Anything you get wrong comes straight back, then again in a week.

Unit test

Every word once, with no feedback until the end. Before each answer is checked you say how sure you were, and the result shows whether feeling sure actually meant being right.
