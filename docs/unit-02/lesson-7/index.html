7.4

Error hunt

Find and correct six errors in this paragraph. Five are comparative-adverb errors; one is a subject–verb agreement slip.

My cousin live in a village near Ninh Binh. She gets up more early than me and she works more hard all day. She rides her bicycle more fast than I can run, and she speaks English more good than her teacher expects. In the evening she studies more careful than anybody in her class.

  1. live → lives (subject–verb agreement)
  2. more early → earlier
  3. more hard → harder
  4. more fast → faster
  5. more good → better
  6. more careful → more carefully

Corrected paragraph: My cousin lives in a village near Ninh Binh. She gets up earlier than me and she works harder all day. She rides her bicycle faster than I can run, and she speaks English better than her teacher expects. In the evening she studies more carefully than anybody in her class.

Project — A village profile

Build a one-page profile of a rural place and compare it with your own.

Step 1 — Choose and collect. Pick a village, hamlet or small town you can find out about: family, neighbours, a photo album, or a short online search. Fill in the table.

Fact Your place The rural place
Population (roughly)
Main work people do
Time to the nearest hospital
Time to the nearest shop
What you can hear at 6 a.m.
What people do in the evening

Step 2 — Compare it. Write six sentences comparing the two places. Every sentence must contain a comparative adverb and than. Use at least one irregular form (better, worse, farther, less).

Step 3 — Draw it. Make a simple map of the rural place: houses, the road, the fields, the water, the school. Label six features in English.

Step 4 — Say it. Present the profile aloud for one minute without reading word for word. Record it. Listen back once and count how many comparative adverbs you actually used.

Stretch: Interview the oldest person you can reach about the same place forty years ago. Write five sentences about what has changed, using comparative adverbs: People travel farther than they did. Children start school earlier than they used to.
