Tí My uncle says one workshop can pour enough into a harbour to kill it before anybody reports it.

Thảo we were small we caught fish there. Now the water looks .

Tí Not any more. As soon as the fish went, the birds stopped coming too.

Thảo Cô Yến is organising a clean-up on Saturday. Our lane could not agree about anything else for a whole week, so she gave us the harbour instead. She says is where every clean-up starts.

Tí Then she'll want . Once we have photographs and dates, she can take them to the district office.

Thảo I'll photograph the water every morning while I walk to school.

Tí And I'll carry the sacks. Bà Sáu says we shouldn't wait until the harbour is completely dead.

Thảo Nobody in this town does anything until it's too late. And Bống? People still stop me about her.

Tí I sent the week back on Sunday night. I stood on our step and said "Go well.", and the wires were live again before breakfast, and half the lane still isn't speaking to the other half.

Thảo So you're finished with it?

Tí The sea gives back what it took — you never choose what. So on Saturday I'm walking her down to the water in front of everybody, and I'm telling her to go home. Then it stops, and it stops where people can see it stop.

Thảo Good. Whenever I walk past that water now, I feel angry.

Tí Keep the anger. Bring gloves on Saturday.

Unit 07 · 28 words

Environmental protection

Ghi chú: Ecosystem rộng hơn habitat: một hệ sinh thái gồm nhiều môi trường sống và cả những thứ không sống (nước, đất, ánh sáng). Một loài chỉ có một habitat, nhưng sống trong cả một ecosystem.

Bẫy thường gặp: participate luôn đi với in: participate in a clean-up. ❌ participate a clean-up. Còn coral vừa đếm được vừa không: a coral reef (rạn san hô) nhưng the coral is dying (san hô nói chung).

Ghi chú: resident /ˈrezɪdənt/ (n) = người dân sống ở một nơi. The residents of this street cleared the canal themselves. Một resident sống ở đó; một visitor chỉ đến rồi đi. Con vật cũng dùng được: the residents of the forest = các loài sống trong rừng.

pollution

/pəˈluːʃn/1

nounsự ô nhiễm

noununcountable

the process of making air, water or land dirty and dangerous, or the substances that do this

sự ô nhiễm

  • Air pollution in the city is getting worse.
  • Factories are a major cause of water pollution.
  • We must reduce pollution from traffic.

air pollution · water pollution · reduce pollution · cause pollution

Word family

  • pollute verb to make air, water or land dirty and unsafe làm ô nhiễmThe factory polluted the river for years.
  • pollutant noun a substance that causes pollution chất gây ô nhiễmCars release several pollutants.

Watch out

Uncountable — never pollutions. Say a lot of pollution or high levels of pollution.

ecosystem

/ˈiːkəʊsɪstəm/19

nounhệ sinh thái

nouncountable

all the living things in one area together with the water, soil and air they depend on, seen as one connected whole

hệ sinh thái

  • A rice field is a small ecosystem of its own.
  • Warmer water is changing the whole ecosystem of the reef.
  • One missing insect can upset a forest ecosystem.

a rich ecosystem · a marine ecosystem · damage an ecosystem · a fragile ecosystem

Watch out

Wider than habitat: a habitat is where one species lives, an ecosystem is the whole set of living and non-living things and the links between them.

carbon dioxide

/ˌkɑːbən daɪˈɒksaɪd/20

nounkhí đi-ô-xít các-bon (CO₂)

noununcountable

the gas that people and animals breathe out and that burning fuel gives off; plants take it in

khí đi-ô-xít các-bon (CO₂)

  • Forests absorb carbon dioxide and give out oxygen.
  • Cars and factories release millions of tonnes of carbon dioxide every year.
  • There is more carbon dioxide in the air now than a century ago.

absorb carbon dioxide · release carbon dioxide · carbon dioxide emissions

Watch out

Two words, no hyphen, and uncountable — never carbon dioxides. Often shortened to CO₂, read aloud as "C-O-two".

carbon footprint

/ˌkɑːbən ˈfʊtprɪnt/21

noundấu chân các-bon (lượng khí thải mình gây ra)

nouncountable

the amount of carbon dioxide that a person, a journey or an activity puts into the environment

dấu chân các-bon (lượng khí thải mình gây ra)

  • Cycling to school cuts your carbon footprint.
  • A flight to Europe has a huge carbon footprint.
  • The club measured the school's carbon footprint for a month.

reduce your carbon footprint · a large carbon footprint · measure a carbon footprint

Watch out

The usual verb is reduce (or cut, or lower) a carbon footprint — not decrease down and not destroy. It belongs to somebody: my/your/the school's carbon footprint.

/ˈkɒrəl/24
