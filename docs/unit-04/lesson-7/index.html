7.4

Error hunt

Find and correct five errors in this paragraph. Two are about question forms; three are about countable and uncountable nouns.

My cousin lives in a stilt house in Cao Bang. How many rooms it has? Only one! Her mother gets up at five and cooks many rice for the whole family. I asked her, "Do you can weave?" She said yes, and she showed me. She told me a lot of informations about her village, and she gave me an advice: be patient.

  1. How many rooms it has? → How many rooms does it have?
  2. many rice → a lot of rice (or: much rice)
  3. Do you can weave? → Can you weave?
  4. a lot of informations → a lot of information
  5. an advice → some advice (or: a piece of advice)

Corrected paragraph: My cousin lives in a stilt house in Cao Bang. How many rooms does it have? Only one! Her mother gets up at five and cooks a lot of rice for the whole family. I asked her, "Can you weave?" She said yes, and she showed me. She told me a lot of information about her village, and she gave me some advice: be patient.

Project — A village fact file

Build a one-page fact file about one ethnic group of Viet Nam.

Step 1 — Choose. Pick one ethnic group you do not already know well. Not your own, if you belong to one.

Step 2 — Write ten questions. Before you look anything up, write ten questions you want answered. Use a different question word each time, and make at least two of them subject questions.

# Your question Answer
1 Where do they mainly live?
2
3
…

Step 3 — Find out. Use a library book, a museum website, or an interview with someone who knows. Write the source next to each answer. If two sources disagree, write both — and say so.

Step 4 — Build the page. One A4 page: a short paragraph of 100–120 words, a picture or a drawing, and your three most interesting facts in a box.

Step 5 — Present it. Talk about your group for one minute without reading word for word. Then invite three questions and answer them.

Stretch: Swap fact files with a classmate. Write five new questions that their page does not answer, and give them back. A good fact file should leave you curious, not finished.
