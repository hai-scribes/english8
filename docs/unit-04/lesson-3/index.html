Grammar — Asking questions

To make a question, English puts a small word in front of the person or thing you are asking about: Is she…? Can he…? Do they…?

1 · Questions you answer with yes or no

If the sentence has is, are, was, can, will, move it to the front. If it has none, put do, does or did in front.

amisarewaswerecanwilldodoesdid

She is Tay. → Is she Tay?The farmers are harvesting rice. → Are the farmers harvesting rice?He can weave. → Can he weave?We will watch a film about the Khmer. → Will we watch a film about the Khmer?They live here. → Do they live here?She lives here. → Does she live here?You visited the village. → Did you visit the village?

After do / does / did, the verb goes back to its plain form: Does she weave? Did you visit?

✗ Does your grandmother weaves baskets?

✗ Did you visited the village last summer?

Short answers use the same small word again:

Is it a stilt house? — Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. Do they grow rice? — Yes, they do. / No, they don't. Can you play the gong? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

Ghi chú: Câu hỏi Yes/No: đưa is / are / can / will lên đầu câu. Nếu câu không có những từ đó, thêm do / does / did — và động từ chính trở về dạng nguyên mẫu (does she weave, không phải weaves).

Put the question word first. After it, the order is the same as in a yes/no question.

whatwhichwherewhenwhywhohowhow manyhow muchhow long

3 · Who lives…? — no do, does or did

When who or what is the one doing the action, keep the order of a normal sentence. There is no do / does / did.

My uncle repairs the roof. → Who repairs the roof?Bamboo makes the walls strong. → What makes the walls strong?Something happened at the festival. → What happened at the festival?

✗ Who does repair the roof?

Asking who does it Asking about someone else
Who lives in the stilt house? Who does she live with?
What happened at the festival? What did you see at the festival?

Bẫy thường gặp: Who lives here? — who là người làm hành động, nên không có does, và động từ vẫn thêm -s. Còn Who does she live with? — she mới là người làm hành động, nên cần does.

4 · Things you can count, and things you can't

Some nouns can be counted: one basket, two baskets. They have a plural.

baskethousesongfactroomstatue

Some cannot be counted: they have no plural -s and never take a / an.

ricefoodwatermusicadviceinformationjewelleryclothing

5 · Counting rice, advice and water

Put a word for the container or the piece in front.

a piece ofa bowl ofa bottle ofa bar ofa loaf of

a piece of advice · a piece of information a bowl of rice · a bottle of water a bar of soap · a loaf of bread

Bẫy thường gặp: Các danh từ information, rice, food, clothing, advice, jewellery không đếm được trong tiếng Anh, dù tiếng Việt vẫn nói
"những/các". ❌ an advice → ✅ a piece of advice · ❌ many rices → ✅ a lot of rice · ❌ many jewelleries → ✅ a lot of jewellery.

Ghi chú: How many / a few đi với danh từ đếm được (baskets).
How much / a little đi với danh từ không đếm được (rice).
