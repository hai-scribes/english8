Communication

Everyday English — Worrying and reassuring

Saying you are worried Nghĩa
I'm a bit worried about the exam. Mình hơi lo về kỳ thi.
I'm not sure I can keep up. Mình không chắc mình theo kịp.
What if I make a mistake? Nhỡ mình làm sai thì sao?
I've got so much on at the moment. Dạo này mình bận quá nhiều thứ.
Reassuring someone Nghĩa
Don't worry — you'll be fine. Đừng lo, bạn sẽ ổn thôi.
It happens to everyone. Ai cũng gặp chuyện đó mà.
You've got this. Bạn làm được mà.
Take it one step at a time. Cứ từ từ từng bước một.
Let me give you a hand. Để mình giúp một tay.

⚠️ Notice how naturally these turn into compound sentences: Don't worry, and don't stay up all night. It happens to everyone, so please stop apologising. You could revise tonight, or you could get some sleep and start early.

Showing interest Nghĩa
Really? Tell me more. Thật à? Kể thêm đi.
That sounds great. Nghe hay đấy.
How did that go? Chuyện đó thế nào rồi?
I know exactly what you mean. Mình hiểu chính xác ý bạn.

4.2

Write your own

A friend says each of these to you. Reply with one compound sentence that reassures them and offers something practical.

  1. "I'm worried nobody will talk to me at the new club." →
  2. "I've got three tests and a project this week." →
  3. "Everyone in my class has a better phone than me." →

Answers will vary. Check that each reply is one compound sentence with a comma before and, but, so, or, or yet, and that it both reassures and suggests something practical. Model for 1: Don't worry, and I'll sit next to you for the first meeting.
