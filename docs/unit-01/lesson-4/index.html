Communication

Everyday English — Suggesting and responding

Making a suggestion Nghĩa
How about going to the cinema? Đi xem phim thì sao?
Why don't we try that new café? Sao chúng ta không thử...?
Shall we meet at ten? Chúng ta gặp lúc 10 giờ nhé?
Do you fancy playing a board game? Bạn có muốn chơi...?

⚠️ Note the forms: How about and fancy take V-ing. Why don't we and Shall we take the bare infinitive.

Accepting Refusing politely
That sounds great. I'd rather not, if that's OK.
Good idea! Sorry, I can't make it.
I'd love to. Maybe another time?
Sure, why not? I'm not really into that.
4.1

Complete the mini-dialogues

  1. A: How about (go) swimming? B: — I love the pool in summer.

  2. A: Why don't we (watch) a movie? B: Sorry, I make it. I'm busy tonight.

  3. A: Do you fancy (play) football? B: I'd not — I hurt my ankle yesterday.

  1. going / That sounds great (or: Good idea / I'd love to)
  2. watch / can't 3. playing / rather
4.2

Write your own

Someone suggests three activities. Write a reply to each — accept two, refuse one politely, and give a reason.

  1. "How about going to the bookshop on Saturday?" →
  2. "Shall we cook lunch together?" →
  3. "Do you fancy watching a three-hour film?" →

Answers will vary. Check that two replies accept and one refuses politely with a reason.
