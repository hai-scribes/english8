7.4

Error hunt

Find and correct six errors in this paragraph. Five are about verb forms after liking/disliking verbs; one is a subject–verb agreement slip.

My cousin enjoy to play football every Saturday. He can't stand to lose, so he practises to shoot for an hour before each match. I don't mind watch him, but I would like doing something else sometimes.

  1. enjoy → enjoys
  2. to play → playing
  3. to lose → losing
  4. to shoot → shooting
  5. watch → watching
  6. would like doing → would like to do

Corrected paragraph: My cousin enjoys playing football every Saturday. He can't stand losing, so he practises shooting for an hour before each match. I don't mind watching him, but I would like to do something else sometimes.

Project — Your leisure profile

Build a one-page profile of how you actually spend your free time.

Step 1 — Track it. For seven days, write down every leisure activity you do and how long it lasts. Be honest — include scrolling on your phone.

Day Activity Time spent
Mon
Tue
…

Step 2 — Total it. Add up the hours for each type of activity: screen · social · physical · creative · reading · other.

Step 3 — Present it. Write a short report of 100–120 words answering:

  • Which activity takes the most of your time? Were you surprised?
  • Which activity do you enjoy the most? Is it the same one?
  • What would you like to take up, and what would you give up to make room?

Step 4 — Say it. Present your profile aloud for one minute, without reading word for word. Record it. Listen back once.

Stretch: Do Step 1 again in three months and compare. Did anything change?
