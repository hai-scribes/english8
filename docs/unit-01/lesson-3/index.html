Grammar — Verbs of liking and disliking

When one verb comes straight after another, the second verb has one of two shapes:

The -ing shape The to shape
reading, cooking, playing to read, to cook, to play

Which shape you use depends on the first verb. There are three groups.

1 · These take the -ing shape

enjoydon't mindcan't standdislikeavoidpractisefancy

I enjoy reading comics.She can't stand waiting in queues.He doesn't mind doing the washing-up.

✗ I enjoy to read.

2 · These take either shape

likelovehateprefer

I love cooking. = I love to cook.They hate getting up early. = They hate to get up early.

Ghi chú: Hai cách nói gần như cùng nghĩa. Ở lớp 8, cả hai đều đúng.

3 · would like, would love, would prefer take the to shape

would likewould lovewould prefer

I would like to try that café.She'd love to take up the guitar.

✗ I would like trying that café.

Bẫy thường gặp: I like swimming. — Tôi thích bơi (nói chung).
I would like to swim. — Tôi muốn đi bơi (bây giờ).

leisure

/ˈleʒə(r)/1

nounthời gian rảnh rỗi

1

noununcountable

time when you are not working or studying, and can do what you enjoy

thời gian rảnh rỗi

  • Most teenagers have very little leisure during exam week.
  • The town has a new leisure centre with a pool and a gym.

2

noununcountable, formal

the state of having time to relax, with no work you must do

Usually sits before another noun: leisure time, leisure activities. The first syllable rhymes with measure, not with seize.
