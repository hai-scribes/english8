A Closer Look 2

Grammar — Verbs of liking and disliking

After verbs like love, like, enjoy, hate, the next verb changes form. There are three groups. Learn which group each verb belongs to.

Group 1 — always + V-ing

enjoy · don't mind · can't stand · dislike · avoid · practise · fancy

I enjoy reading comics. She can't stand waiting in queues. He doesn't mind doing the washing-up.

❌ I enjoy to read. — never correct.

Group 2 — both, with (almost) no change in meaning

like · love · hate · prefer

I love cooking. = I love to cook. They hate getting up early. = They hate to get up early.

Ghi chú: Với nhóm này, V-ing thường nói về sở thích chung, còn to-V thường nói về lựa chọn trong một tình huống cụ thể. Ở lớp 8, cả hai đều được chấp nhận.

Group 3 — would + like/love/prefer → always to-V

would like · would love · would prefer

I would like to try that café. (= I want to, now) She 'd love to take up the guitar.

❌ I would like trying that café. — never correct.

⚠️ Bẫy thường gặp: like + V-ing (sở thích chung) nhưng would like + to-V (mong muốn cụ thể). So sánh: I like swimming. — Tôi thích bơi (nói chung). I would like to swim. — Tôi muốn đi bơi (bây giờ).
