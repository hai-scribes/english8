A Closer Look 2

Grammar — Verbs of liking and disliking

After verbs like love, like, enjoy, hate, the next verb changes form. There are three groups. Learn which group each verb belongs to.

Group 1 — always + V-ing

enjoy · don't mind · can't stand · dislike · avoid · practise · fancy

I enjoy reading comics. She can't stand waiting in queues. He doesn't mind doing the washing-up.

❌ I enjoy to read. — never correct.

Group 2 — both, with (almost) no change in meaning

like · love · hate · prefer

I love cooking. = I love to cook. They hate getting up early. = They hate to get up early.

Ghi chú: Với nhóm này, V-ing thường nói về sở thích chung, còn to-V thường nói về lựa chọn trong một tình huống cụ thể. Ở lớp 8, cả hai đều được chấp nhận.

Group 3 — would + like/love/prefer → always to-V

would like · would love · would prefer

I would like to try that café. (= I want to, now) She 'd love to take up the guitar.

❌ I would like trying that café. — never correct.

⚠️ Bẫy thường gặp: like + V-ing (sở thích chung) nhưng would like + to-V (mong muốn cụ thể). So sánh: I like swimming. — Tôi thích bơi (nói chung). I would like to swim. — Tôi muốn đi bơi (bây giờ).

3.2

One form only

Put the verb in brackets into the correct form. Only one answer is possible.

  1. I can't stand (queue) for tickets.
  2. She'd like (join) the chess club.
  3. My brother avoids (cook) whenever he can.
  4. We don't mind (walk) if it isn't far.
  5. Would you prefer (stay) in tonight?
  6. He practises (play) the piano every evening.
  1. queuing 2. to join 3. cooking 4. walking 5. to stay 6. playing
3.3

Two forms possible

Write the sentence twice — once with V-ing, once with to-V.

  1. I / love / watch / films with my dad - -
  2. She / hate / get up / before seven - -
  1. I love watching films with my dad. / I love to watch films with my dad.
  2. She hates getting up before seven. / She hates to get up before seven.

Correct the mistake

Each sentence has one error. Rewrite it correctly.

  1. I would like going to the new bookshop.
  2. My sister enjoys to make paper crafts.
  3. They can't stand to be late.
  4. He doesn't mind to help with the cooking.
  5. Would you love watching a movie tonight?

3.5

About you

Write true sentences about yourself. Use a different verb each time.

  1. (enjoy)
  2. (can't stand)
  3. (would love)
  4. (don't mind)

Answers will vary. Check: enjoy + V-ing, can't stand + V-ing, would love + to-V, don't mind + V-ing.
