Project — Your leisure profile

Build a one-page profile of how you actually spend your free time.

Step 1 — Track it. For seven days, write down every leisure activity you do and how long it lasts. Be honest — include scrolling on your phone.

Day Activity Time spent
Mon
Tue
…

Step 2 — Total it. Add up the hours for each type of activity: screen · social · physical · creative · reading · other.

Step 3 — Present it. Write a short report of 100–120 words answering:

  • Which activity takes the most of your time? Were you surprised?
  • Which activity do you enjoy the most? Is it the same one?
  • What would you like to take up, and what would you give up to make room?

Step 4 — Say it. Present your profile aloud for one minute, without reading word for word. Record it. Listen back once.

Stretch: Do Step 1 again in three months and compare. Did anything change?

leisure

/ˈleʒə(r)/1

nounthời gian rảnh rỗi

1

noununcountable

time when you are not working or studying, and can do what you enjoy

thời gian rảnh rỗi

  • In her leisure time, my sister does jigsaw puzzles.
  • Most teenagers have very little leisure during exam week.
  • The town has a new leisure centre with a pool and a gym.

leisure time · leisure activities · a leisure centre

2

noununcountable, formal

the state of having time to relax, with no work you must do

sự nhàn hạ, sự thảnh thơi

  • He retired early and now lives a life of leisure.
  • Read the contract at your leisure and sign it next week.

a life of leisure · at your leisure

Word family

  • leisurely adjective done slowly, without hurrying thong thả, thư tháiWe had a leisurely breakfast and left at ten.

Watch out

Usually sits before another noun: leisure time, leisure activities. The first syllable rhymes with measure, not with seize.

pastime

/ˈpɑːstaɪm/2

nounthú tiêu khiển, trò giải trí

nouncountable

something you do regularly for pleasure when you are not working

thú tiêu khiển, trò giải trí

  • Fishing is his favourite pastime.
  • Board games are a cheap pastime for a rainy Sunday.
  • Collecting stamps was a popular pastime fifty years ago.

a favourite pastime · a popular pastime · a national pastime

Watch out

One word, one s — not past time. Close to hobby, but a pastime can be more casual: a hobby usually needs equipment or skill.

Informal. Use with for people and at / in for places: hang out with Nam at the café. Irregular: hang → hung → hung.

socialise

/ˈsəʊʃəlaɪz/4

verbgiao lưu, gặp gỡ mọi người

verbintransitive

to meet and spend time with other people in a friendly way

giao lưu, gặp gỡ mọi người

  • I don't socialise much during the week because of homework.
  • A club is a good place to socialise with people your own age.

Word family

  • social adjective connected with meeting people and spending time with them thuộc về xã hội, giao tiếpShe has a busy social life.

board game

/ˈbɔːd ɡeɪm/11

a game such as chess, played on a marked board with pieces or counters

Two words. Board /bɔːd/ sounds exactly like bored — same sound, completely different word. You play a board game, but you do a jigsaw puzzle.

to take part in a game or physical activity such as football or badminton

chơi thể thao

  • Tom and his friends play sport in the park every Saturday.
  • Tom is keen on playing sport — football, badminton, anything outdoors.
  • I play sport three times a week to stay in shape.
  • She doesn't play sport, but she cycles everywhere.

playing sport · play sport at the weekend · play a lot of sport

Watch out

British English says play sport with no article; American English usually says play sports. Both are understood.

vòng đeo tay

  • My favourite leisure activity is DIY — I make paper flowers and bracelets.
  • She gave me a bracelet she had made herself.
  • He wears a thin silver bracelet on his left wrist.

judo

/ˈdʒuːdəʊ/36

nounvõ judo

noununcountable

a sport from Japan in which two people try to throw each other to the ground

võ judo

  • My friends are keen on judo and they go to a judo club every Sunday.
  • She has done judo since she was seven.
  • Judo teaches you how to fall without hurting yourself.

do judo · a judo club · a judo lesson

Watch out

Like most martial arts, judo takes do, not play: I do judo, never ❌ I play judo.
