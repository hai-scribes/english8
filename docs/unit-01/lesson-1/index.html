leisure

/ˈleʒə(r)/1

nounthời gian rảnh rỗi

1

noununcountable

time when you are not working or studying, and can do what you enjoy

thời gian rảnh rỗi

  • In her leisure time, my sister does jigsaw puzzles.
  • Most teenagers have very little leisure during exam week.
  • The town has a new leisure centre with a pool and a gym.

leisure time · leisure activities · a leisure centre

2

noununcountable, formal

the state of having time to relax, with no work you must do

sự nhàn hạ, sự thảnh thơi

  • He retired early and now lives a life of leisure.
  • Read the contract at your leisure and sign it next week.

a life of leisure · at your leisure

Word family

  • leisurely adjective done slowly, without hurrying thong thả, thư tháiWe had a leisurely breakfast and left at ten.

Watch out

Usually sits before another noun: leisure time, leisure activities. The first syllable rhymes with measure, not with seize.

pastime

/ˈpɑːstaɪm/2

nounthú tiêu khiển, trò giải trí

nouncountable

something you do regularly for pleasure when you are not working

thú tiêu khiển, trò giải trí

  • Fishing is his favourite pastime.
  • Board games are a cheap pastime for a rainy Sunday.
  • Collecting stamps was a popular pastime fifty years ago.

a favourite pastime · a popular pastime · a national pastime

Watch out

One word, one s — not past time. Close to hobby, but a pastime can be more casual: a hobby usually needs equipment or skill.

socialise

/ˈsəʊʃəlaɪz/4

verbgiao lưu, gặp gỡ mọi người

verbintransitive

  • I don't socialise much during the week because of homework.
  • A club is a good place to socialise with people your own age.

Word family

board game

/ˈbɔːd ɡeɪm/11

nounnhà búp bê

noun

a toy house, small enough to hold, with tiny rooms and furniture inside

nhà búp bê

  • In my leisure time I love knitting and building dollhouses.
  • He built a wooden dollhouse for his little sister.
  • The dollhouse has four rooms and a staircase.
