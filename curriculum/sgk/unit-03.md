## 3 · A Closer Look 2 — Simple and compound sentences (book p. 31)

> **Remember!**
> A **simple sentence** is made up of one independent clause.
> *Minh has some problems with his schoolwork.*
>
> A **compound sentence** is made up of two or more independent clauses, joined by:
> - a **coordinating conjunction**: *for, and, but, or, so*
>   *Mai bought many books, **for** she likes reading.*
> - a **conjunctive adverb**: *however, therefore, otherwise*
>   *Mark is hard-working; **therefore**, he usually gets high scores on exams.*

Note the two devices are equally weighted here, and the conjunctive adverbs take
a **semicolon** before and a **comma** after.

| Ex | Type | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tick the simple sentences | Five sentences, three of them compound (joined by *and*) |
| 2 | Write **S** or **C** | Five sentences to classify |
| 3 | Three-option MCQ, conjunction or conjunctive adverb | 1 *…freedom, ___ they should also set limits* (**but**) · 2 *We don't cheat on exams, ___ it is a wrong thing to do* (**for**) · 3 *Lan wants to join the music club; ___, she can't sing or play any instruments* (**however**) · 4 *Schoolwork causes pressure, ___ they also feel pressure from their parents* (**and**) · 5 *She wanted to prepare for the exam; ___, she turned off her mobile phone* (**therefore**) |
| 4 | Combine pairs of sentences | Box: *or · but · therefore · otherwise · so* — five pairs (Phong must study harder / may fail; she is sensitive / don't comment on her hairstyle; Mi wants more friends / doesn't connect well; students can work in groups / in pairs; likes showing off / often posts pictures) |
| 5 | **GAME** — Which group has the most sentences? | Groups make as many compound sentences as possible; most correct wins |

## 5 · Skills 1 (book pp. 33–34)

### Reading — a conversation about school clubs

**Ex 1** — pair lead-in: (a) name the club in each picture (chess, arts and
crafts); (b) which of your school clubs do you want to join?

**Text.** A dialogue between Mai and Tom, ~230 words. Tom is interested in two
clubs, **badminton** and **chess**. Badminton is relaxing; he plays it to keep
fit. It meets Tuesdays and Fridays 5:00–6:30 p.m., starting 30 minutes after
school, so there is time for snacks beforehand. Tom started chess five years
ago; his mum first sent him to a chess club because she wanted him to be more
**focused**, and he now finds he can **concentrate better**. Mai wants to join
the new **arts and crafts club**, where members work together in small
**community service projects**, do art projects, and improve their **practical
skills and teamwork skills**. **Ms Hoa**, the art teacher, will coach it and
help them connect with the community.
