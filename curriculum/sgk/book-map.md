# Book Map and unit targets

The book's own Book Map (pp. 4–7), joined with each unit's *This unit includes*
box (the first page of every unit). Together these are the seven official
targets per unit: vocabulary, pronunciation, grammar, Everyday English, and the
four skills.

Contents order: Units 1–3 · **Review 1** · Units 4–6 · **Review 2** ·
Units 7–9 · **Review 3** · Units 10–12 · **Review 4** · Glossary.

Every writing task in the book is **80–100 words**, without exception,
including Unit 7's notice and Unit 9's instructions.

## The shape every unit takes

Seven sections over ten pages, in this order:

| Section | Pages | What it contains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Getting Started** | 2 | A titled dialogue (*Listen and read*, with a track number), then 4 exercises: comprehension, a picture/word-labelling set, a gap-fill from a word box, and a pair/group speaking prompt. Often closes with a **GAME** or **QUIZ**. |
| **A Closer Look 1** | 1 | Vocabulary (3 exercises) then Pronunciation (2): *listen and repeat, then sort the words into two columns*, and *listen and practise the sentences, underline X and circle Y*. |
| **A Closer Look 2** | 2 | Grammar, opening with a yellow **Remember!** box stating the rule, then 4–5 exercises ending in a GAME or a personalised *complete the sentences about yourself*. |
| **Communication** | 2 | **Everyday English** — a highlighted model dialogue and a pair task — *and* a separate named **content block** with 2–3 exercises, usually cultural or cross-cultural. |
| **Skills 1** | 2 | Reading (a lead-in, a text with 2–3 exercise types) → Speaking (fact-files or a notes table, then report to the class). |
| **Skills 2** | 2 | Listening (a lead-in, then 2 tasks on one recording) → Writing (an idea-organising task, then the 80–100 word task with a printed opening frame). |
| **Looking Back + Project** | 2 | Vocabulary (2) and Grammar (2) revision, a group Project ending in a poster or presentation, and the **Now I can …** self-assessment table. |

### The "Now I can …" table

Closes every unit. Eight statements — one per target — each rated in one of
three columns: ✓ / ✓✓ / ✓✓✓. The statements are the Book Map targets rewritten
in the first person, e.g. Unit 4:

- use the words related to the lifestyle of ethnic groups.
- pronounce the sounds /k/ and /ɡ/ correctly in words and sentences.
- recognise and use *Yes/No* and *Wh*-questions, and countable and uncountable nouns.
- give opinions.
- read about stilt houses.
- talk about the type of home I live in.
- listen about the life of minority children.
- write a paragraph about the things I do to help my family.
