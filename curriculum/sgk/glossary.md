# The book's glossary (pp. 136–139)

282 headwords, by unit, with the book's own IPA and Vietnamese gloss.

This is the lexis a grade-8 learner is examined on. It is **not** everything the
book teaches — each unit's *A Closer Look 1* and *Getting Started* drill further
words and phrases that are never glossed here (club types, the countryside
activity phrases, the natural-disaster names, the eight planets). Those are
recorded in the per-unit files.

Abbreviations as printed: `adj` adjective · `adv` adverb · `con` conjunction ·
`n` noun · `pre` preposition · `v` verb.
