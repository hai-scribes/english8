## 1 · Getting Started — "I'm from the Tay ethnic group." (track 20)

**Dialogue.** Tom meets Lai, who is new and comes from **Ha Giang**. She is from
the **Tay** ethnic group — *"the second largest ethnic group in Viet Nam, only
after the Kinh"*. Tom has seen a bamboo house on high posts in a travel
brochure; Lai confirms it is a **stilt house**, and hers **overlooks terraced
fields**. Her village has **16 houses** and is peaceful, close to nature. Her
culture shows in **folk dances**, **musical instruments** like the *đàn tính*,
and **five-colour sticky rice**.

## 5 · Skills 1 (book pp. 45–46)

### Reading — "Stilt houses"

**Ex 1** — label three pictures from a box: *open fire · posts · staircase*.

**Text.** Three paragraphs, ~180 words. Stilt houses are popular among many
ethnic minority groups, from the **Thai** in the Northern Highlands to the
**Khmer** in the Mekong Delta. They come in different sizes and styles and show
the traditional culture of their owners. They are made from natural materials —
wood, bamboo, leaves — and stand on strong **posts** about two or three metres
above the ground, which keeps people safe from wild animals. People climb a
**seven- or nine-step staircase** to enter. The most important place is the
kitchen, which has an **open fire** in the middle and is the place for family
gatherings and receiving guests. Tay and Nung stilt houses usually **overlook a
field**; Thai stilt houses face **mountains or a forest**. The **Bahnar and Ede**
have a communal house — a ***Rong* house** — as the heart of the village, the
largest and tallest in the village.

| Ex | Type | Items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | True / False, four statements | Only a few minority groups live in stilt houses (F) · All stilt houses look alike (F) · Family gatherings take place by the open fire in the middle of the house · The *Rong* house serves as the centre of an Ede village |
| 3 | Gap-fill, **ONE word** | A stilt house shows the ___ culture of the owner · The Tay's and Nung's stilt houses overlook a ___ · The Thai's stilt houses overlook a ___ or ___ · The largest and tallest house in an Ede village is the ___ house |

### Speaking — the type of home you live in

**Ex 4** — pair: tick the features of a stilt house from a six-item list, three
of which are false: *has a flat cement roof · stands on big posts · is made from
