## 1 · Getting Started — "Last summer holiday" (track 7)

**Dialogue.** Nick notices Mai's tan. She has come back from three weeks at her
uncle's house in a small village in **Bac Giang Province**. It was **harvest
time**: villagers were harvesting rice with a **combine harvester**; she helped
**load** the rice onto a truck, then **unloaded** and **dried** it. She went with
the village children to **herd** the buffaloes and cows and made friends on her
first day. They took her to the **paddy fields** to fly kites, and in the evening
played traditional games — **bamboo dancing** and **dragon-snake**. Mai's closing
line is the unit's thesis: *"Things move more slowly there than in our city, but
people seem to have a healthier life."*

## 3 · A Closer Look 2 — Comparative adverbs (book p. 21)

> **Remember!**
> - For most adverbs (often with two or more syllables) we make comparatives
>   with **more**: *slowly → more slowly · carefully → more carefully*
> - For adverbs with the same form as adjectives — *fast, hard, soon* — we add
>   **-er**: *fast → faster · hard → harder*
> - Some irregular adverbs: *well → **better*** · *badly → **worse***

| Ex | Type | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Table completion | *long (→ longer, given) · high · late · quickly · frequently · early · much · little* |
| 2 | Gap-fill from brackets | Mai dances (beautifully) · write (clearly) · life in the city moves (fast) · you must work much (hard) · today it's raining (heavily) |
| 3 | Gap-fill from a box | Box: *early · soundly · fast · carefully · quietly* — driving after an accident; a horse vs a buffalo; speak a bit ___; slept ___ than ever before; started harvesting ___ than expected |

## 5 · Skills 1 (book p. 24)

### Reading — life in a village in southern Viet Nam

**Ex 1** — lead-in on a picture, plus: which of these adjectives describe it?
*peaceful · vast · crowded · picturesque · noisy*

**Text.** First-person, four paragraphs, ~190 words. The writer feels fortunate
to live in a peaceful village in southern Viet Nam: beautiful and picturesque
scenery, vast fields **stretching** long distances, houses surrounded by green
trees, lakes, ponds and **canals**, fresh cool air. Life moves more slowly than
in cities but people work very hard — growing vegetables, **cultivating** rice,
raising cattle, using combine harvesters at harvest time. Many families live by
growing fruit trees in the **orchards**; others live by fishing in lakes, ponds
and canals. Life is comfortable for children: they play traditional games and
sometimes help their parents pick fruit and herd cattle. People know each other
well, are friendly and hospitable, and often meet in the evening — eating fruit,
playing chess, singing folk songs and chatting about everyday activities.

| Ex | Type | Items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Match four highlighted words to meanings | **stretching** = covering a large area of land · **canals** = small passages used for carrying water to fields, crops · **cultivate** = to grow plants or crops · **orchards** = places where people grow fruit trees |
| 3 | True / False, five statements | Life is very peaceful · The people work very hard · Villagers live **only** by catching fish (F) · The children are **always** busy helping their parents (F) · The villagers get along well |

## 6 · Skills 2 (book p. 25)

### Listening — three people on life in the countryside (tracks 11, 12)

**Ex 1** — lead-in: what do you like or dislike about life in the countryside?

**Ex 2** — **matching speakers to opinions.** Three speakers, three opinions
A–C:
- **A** I don't like rural life because there are not many good schools and colleges. And entertainment and means of transport are very poor.
- **B** I like rural life because the people here are close to each other.
- **C** I want to live in the countryside because there are many things I can't easily do in the city.

**Ex 3** — five MCQs on the same recording: a great sense of ___ in speaker 1's
village (security / duty / **community**) · people in his village are very ___
(**supportive** / unsociable / well-trained) · speaker 2 thinks ___ is a big
problem (cost of living / safety / **transportation**) · speaker 2 says they
should ___ · according to speaker 3, life is peaceful and ___ (slow / **simple**
/ safe).

### Writing — a paragraph on rural life

**Ex 4** — group: fill a two-column LIKE / DISLIKE table. Seeded with
*low cost of living · many cheap things* / *not many entertainment places, such
as theatres, cinemas, etc. · poor means of transport*.

**Ex 5** — write a paragraph of **80–100 words** about what you like **OR**
dislike about life in the countryside. Opening frame printed:
*There are some things I like / dislike about life in the countryside. ______*
