## 1 · Getting Started — "At the Go Green Club" (track 40)

**Dialogue.** A club leader, Nam and Ann discuss environmental problems and
protection. Serious problems named: **pollution and habitat loss**; air and water
quality getting worse; **global warming**; **endangered species loss**. Solutions:
**reduce our carbon footprint** even at home; **turn off devices** when not in
use; **practise the 3Rs**; **plant more trees** in the neighbourhood; **avoid
using single-use products**, like plastic bags, and **stop littering**;
**volunteer** at local environment programmes to save endangered species.

Ann asks *"What do you mean by 'carbon footprint'?"* — which is the unit's
Everyday English function planted in the opening dialogue. The leader defines it:
**the amount of carbon dioxide we release into the environment**.

## 3 · A Closer Look 2 — Adverb clauses of time (book p. 75)

> **Remember!**
> - A **complex sentence** contains one independent clause and at least one
>   dependent clause. *The roads were slippery **when it rained**.*
> - An **adverb clause** is a dependent clause.
> - An adverb clause of time shows **when** something happens, and is usually
>   introduced by time connectors: ***before, after, when, while, till / until,
>   as soon as, …***
>   ***As soon as** they arrive, we'll have lunch.*

| Ex | Type | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Write **I.C** or **D.C** | Five sentences with one clause underlined; worked example given |
| 2 | Three-option MCQ on the connector | *I was not at home ___ he came to see me* (**when**) · *We called the forest guard unit ___ we saw the bush fire* (**as soon as**) · *I went straight to the gym ___ I left home* (**after**) · *You can stay with us ___ you find a suitable place to stay* (**until**) · *We cleaned up everything at the campsite ___ we left* (**before**) |
| 3 | Match clauses into complex sentences | *We will clean up the beach* → **as soon as it stops raining** · *Don't forget to turn off the lights* → **before you go out** · *You have to show your ticket* → **when you get on the train** · *Can you help me lay the table* → **while I'm cooking the soup?** · *Thomas Edison kept trying* → **until he made a successful light bulb** |
| 4 | Combine pairs with the given conjunction | *(as soon as)* · *(when)* · *(before)* · *(while)* · *(After)* |
| 5 | **GAME** — Matching game | Group A writes main clauses, Group B writes adverb clauses of time. Do they match? Are there any funny sentences? |

**Ex 3** — a passage and a tick task. Facts: Earth Day is celebrated on **April
22nd** by more than **190 countries**; the first Earth Day started in **1970 in

## 5 · Skills 1 (book pp. 77–78)

### Reading — Con Dao National Park

**Ex 1** — group lead-in: say what you see in the picture (a sea turtle), then
list the names of some endangered species you know.

**Text.** Three paragraphs, ~180 words. There are national parks all over the
world and the number is rising; a national park is a special area **for the
protection of the environment and wildlife**. In Viet Nam there are now **34
national parks**. Con Dao became a national park in **1993**. It is in Con Dao
District, **Ba Ria–Vung Tau Province**, and contains **16 small islands covering
20,000 hectares**. Its **ecosystem** is very diverse with thousands of species,
including marine animals — many species of **corals**, as well as **sea turtles,
dolphins, and endangered dugongs**. The park is home to valuable kinds of woods
and **medicinal plants**; three ancient trees there were named **"Vietnamese
Heritage Trees"**. Like other national parks it plays a key role in saving
endangered species and protecting the environment and natural resources, and
helps raise local residents' awareness of the importance of nature.

| Ex | Type | Items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Choose the correct word to make the statement true | *There are 34 **national parks** / nations' parks in Viet Nam now* · *The conservation / **ecosystem** in Con Dao is very diverse* · *The dugong is a(n) dangerous / **endangered** animal* · *National parks play a key role in saving the **environment** / small islands* |
| 3 | Five MCQs, incl. **best title** and **NOT true** | 1 What is the best title for the passage? (**20,000 hectares**) · 4 Con Dao has many kinds of ___ (**valuable plants**) · 5 Which of the following is **NOT** true? (**Con Dao National Park's mission is to help other national parks**) |
