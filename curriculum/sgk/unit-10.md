## 3 · A Closer Look 2 — Prepositions and possessive pronouns (book pp. 107–108)

Two grammar points.

### Prepositions of place

**Ex 1** — complete with *at, in, in front of, on, opposite,* or *under*:
*Lily's house is ___ the end of this street* · *players always sit ___ each other
in a chess game* · *she looked ___ the table and finally found her smartwatch* ·
*don't walk ___ the street. Walk ___ the pavement* · *Ann stood ___ me in a line
to get on the bus*

### Prepositions of time: *in, for, by*

> **Remember!**
> - "**in** + a length of time" can express **future** meaning.
>   *Robots will replace human shop assistants **in ten years**.*
> - "**for** + a length of time" says **how long** something goes on for.
>   *It rained **for three hours** yesterday.*
> - "**by** + a specific time" means **"not later than that time"**.
>   *We'll be there **by 6 p.m.***

**Ex 2** — circle the correct preposition, five items.
**Ex 3** — cloze about future smartphones, using *in, on, at, for, by* — **each
preposition only ONCE**.

### Possessive pronouns

> **Remember!** We can use possessive pronouns **after *of***.
> *Mi is one of my friends.* → *Mi is a friend **of mine**.*

**Ex 4** — pair: agree or disagree with two ideas (*We should not use our
smartphones for more than a few hours every day* · *By 2050, the way people
communicate with each other will be different from now*).

**Ex 5** — **sentence transformation**, second sentence same meaning, five items:
*Jack is one of her cousins.* → *Jack is a ___* · *Is this one of his tablets?* ·
*Can I borrow a pencil of yours?* · *Are you one of their relatives?* → *Are
you ___* · *Last year, two of our classmates won scholarships to the US.*

## 5 · Skills 1 (book pp. 109–110)

### Reading — "TELEPATHY", an interview

**Ex 1** — pair: list as many ways of communication as you can. Examples given:
*meeting face to face, sending letters, chatting online, etc.*

**Text.** An MC interviews two Technology Club members, Minh and Tom, ~230 words.
Minh and his friends **mostly text each other** and also **send voice messages**;
Tom **often sees his friends in person**, but sometimes they **call via the
Internet**. Asked whether these will still be popular in 50 years, Tom says no —
they will use more advanced ways, like **telepathy**, passing thoughts to another
person **without talking**. The MC interrupts (*"Hold on."*) to say only a very
few people may have this ability; Minh replies that in the future **everyone**
will be able to use telepathy, wearing **a tiny device to catch our thoughts and
send them to other people**. Problems raised: telepathy devices can **"read" one's
mind**, so **bad people might take advantage of it to control someone else**; and
the MC adds that **some people will be too lazy to even talk anymore**.

| Ex | Type | Items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | **Who says that …?** — tick Minh or Tom | he sends voice messages to friends · he calls his friends by using the Internet · everyone will be able to use telepathy in the future · people need a small device to send their thoughts to others · bad people can make use of telepathy to harm others |
| 3 | Five MCQs incl. a **NOT-mentioned** item | 1 What is the interview mainly about? (**One way of future communication**) · 2 **Which way of communication is NOT mentioned?** (Meeting face to face / **Writing an email** / Telepathy) · 3 According to Tom, telepathy means ___ (**communicating by thoughts**) · 4 The MC says that ___ (**not all people can do telepathy**) · 5 What does the MC think may be a problem with telepathy? (**Some people won't want to talk anymore**) |
