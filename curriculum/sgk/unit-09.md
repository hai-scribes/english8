## 1 · Getting Started — "We are all safe!" (track 53)

**Dialogue.** Mi is sad: her uncle called this morning — their home town has been
affected by a **flood**, the second time this year. Her uncle, his wife and
children are all safe; they **moved everything to the second floor** of their
house last night. Tom's home town in the US sometimes has **tornadoes**. Mi asks
what a tornado is: *"It's a violent storm that moves in a circle with very strong
winds."* Tom remembers one from last year — one evening his parents and he **were
having dinner** when suddenly they heard a **very loud noise**; looking out of the
window they saw a **big funnel of wind** moving towards them. It **damaged the
roof** of their house and **pulled up some trees** in the yard; fortunately no one
was hurt.

The past-continuous-interrupted-by-past-simple pattern is planted here.

## 3 · A Closer Look 2 — The past continuous (book p. 95)

> **Remember!** We use the past continuous to describe:
> - an action that was happening at a **particular time in the past**.
>   *I **was having** dinner at 6 p.m. yesterday.* · *Were you having dinner at
>   6 p.m. yesterday? — Yes, I was.*
> - a past action that was happening **when another action interrupted it**. We
>   use the **past simple** for the action that interrupted.
>   *When / While we **were watching** TV, we **felt** the earthquake.*
>   *What **were** they **doing** when they felt the earthquake? — They were
>   watching TV.*
>
> **Notes:** We can use ***when* or *while*** before the past continuous.
> We can only use ***when*** before the past simple.

| Ex | Type | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Verbs in brackets → past continuous | They (help) the flood victims at 10 o'clock last night · It (not snow) when I left home · While she (work) in the field, the tornado came · What ___ you (do) at 9 o'clock yesterday morning? — I (watch) the news about a volcanic eruption · ___ you (cry) when I saw you two days ago? — No, I wasn't |
| 2 | Circle past simple or past continuous | Five contrasting pairs, incl. *After Tom **ran** / was running out of his house, he moved / **was moving** quickly to a safer place* |
| 3 | Picture-cued writing | Write what each person in Lan's family was doing when the earthquake happened, from a box: *watch TV · read a book · talk on the phone · draw · drink tea* |
| 4 | Form questions, then pair Q&A | Worked example: *you / play / football / 5 o'clock yesterday afternoon?* → *Were you playing football at 5 o'clock yesterday afternoon?* Three more cues |
| 5 | **GAME** — Memory challenge | Groups take turns saying a sentence describing what each person in a classroom picture was doing |

## 5 · Skills 1 (book pp. 97–98)

### Reading — two news articles

**Ex 1** — match two headlines to two disasters: *"30 seconds of a slight shaking
in Ha Noi"* → **earthquake** · *"A thick layer of ash covers Tonga"* → **volcanic
eruption**

**Text 1 — Tonga.** A volcano in the South Pacific **erupted violently** last
Saturday, hitting **Tonga**, an island country in the area. The eruption sent a
cloud of **ash and gas** into the air, visible from **20 kilometres** away. It
also caused a **tsunami** which flooded properties in Tonga's capital, and
destroyed **hundreds of homes** on some small islands. More than **twenty people**
on these islands are still **missing**. New Zealand sent two big ships to help
the victims yesterday.

**Text 2 — Ha Noi.** Residents in tall buildings in Ha Noi were frightened when
they felt a slight shaking for about **30 seconds** last night. *"I was watching
TV when my building started **trembling**. Books, lights, and other things also
moved"*, said Ms Nguyen Ha, a resident in the **Sunshine Building**. Many people
living in the building ran out of their homes in **fear**. According to
scientists, a **strong earthquake in China** caused this shaking. Luckily, there
was **no damage**.
