learn-anywhere

She signs in once with the Google account she already has, and from then on her work follows her: every device she signs in on shows the same progress, the same daily and weekly record of what she has done, and the same state she left. It stays instant — every interaction answers from local storage, never from a round trip — and it keeps working with no network at all, because a learner on a phone is regularly on no network. Everything the app does on her behalf is visible while it happens: there is no moment where the page is talking to a server and not saying so.
