Atelier · prototype lane

learn-anywhere

She signs in once with the Google account she already has, and from then on her work follows her: every device she signs in on shows the same progress, the same daily and weekly record of what she has done, and the same state she left. It stays instant — every interaction answers from local storage, never from a round trip — and it keeps working with no network at all, because a learner on a phone is regularly on no network. Everything the app does on her behalf is visible while it happens: there is no moment where the page is talking to a server and not saying so.

m2 Her work follows her, and only hers COMPLETE

correctness_callstatus: completeobjective: Work done on one device appears on another after signing in as the same learner, and work done offline reaches a third device once the network returns. Equally: a different account sees none of it. What she sees on one device is what she sees on the other: a task shown done on one is shown done on the other, with the same answers, marks and attempt history, without her doing anything but sign in. How that state is stored is the implementation's choice; there is no older format to preserve.risk: Two risks, and the second is the serious one. First, app.js reads its state once, at boot, and paints from it — work that arrives from another device afterwards is not on the page until something paints it again, so a build that pulls work into storage and never shows it looks finished and is not. m4 Nothing happens that she cannot see, and nothing needs the network COMPLETE

correctness_callstatus: completeobjective: Every network request the page makes is visible in the UI while it is in flight: the sync indicator names its state, carries readable text about when it last succeeded, and says so when it fails.
