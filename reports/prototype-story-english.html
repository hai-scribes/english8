Atelier · prototype lane

story-english

A responsive web app - phone and desktop, fast, offline-capable - that a child opens on a weekday with no help and learns English from, and that gives her uncle something real to look at on Saturday. Underneath it, three architectures designed rather than inherited: (1) a TEACHING architecture whose atomic unit is a short session rather than a lesson, delivering every prescribed target while free to sequence and pace them as the evidence directs; (2) a MARKING architecture that is local, synchronous and offline - question types engineered toward closed answer spaces, official key grammar carrying legitimate variation, two severities so coursework is not marked with test severity, and a near-miss state so a nearly-right answer is neither credited nor accused; (3) an APP architecture with real module boundaries, content as data, versioned local state, and asset slots that make a drawing work the moment it is dropped in. Her underlying English improves, which is the honest route to any IELTS improvement; the app never outputs, predicts or promises a band.
